# Compatible Extensions

Parousia aims to provide compatibility with existing browser extensions and integrations that use Rich Presence bridges.

Compatibility is an ongoing goal. If an integration works with Parousia, it can be added here.

## MAL-Sync

Integrates MyAnimeList with various websites, including automatic episode tracking and Rich Presence support.

**Status:** Implemented, off until someone turns it on (Settings > Platforms > Other extensions).

How MAL-Sync reaches Discord, from its source (`src/index-webextension/serviceworker.ts`, `src/pages-sync/syncPage.ts`) and the shipped Firefox build: it never talks to Discord-RPC-Extension's app or its port 6969. With its own "Discord Rich Presence" setting on, its content script registers with Discord-RPC-Extension's *browser extension* (`runtime.sendMessage(<their id>, { mode: "active" })`), and that extension asks MAL-Sync's background for the tab's presence every 15 seconds with a cross-extension `{ tab, info }` message. MAL-Sync relays it to the tab's content script and returns `{ clientId, presence }`, or `{}` where the page isn't one it recognized. Its background checks neither who asks nor its own Discord setting, and its manifest has no `externally_connectable`, so any extension can ask.

Parousia asks the same way, for the one tab it is sharing, and uses the reply as that tab's Activity, so it reaches Discord through whichever route Parousia already has: Parousia Desktop when it's connected, Discord-RPC-Extension's app otherwise, and never both, because Discord-RPC-Extension's link still steps aside while Desktop is connected. Parousia sends MAL-Sync nothing but a tab number, registers nowhere, and listens for nothing new. It adds no permission and no `externally_connectable` entry. MAL-Sync's own detection is used as it is; Parousia adds none for it.

- **Where it applies.** On a page MAL-Sync recognizes (its supported anime and manga sites and any custom domains), its reply replaces what the site's own Activity would show, and while it has none the site's own Activity (or the Default Activity) shows as usual. It needs the MAL-Sync extension installed. Parousia knows MAL-Sync by its published ids: the Chrome Web Store's, and the one on addons.mozilla.org (the Firefox id in MAL-Sync's repository differs, and isn't the one its store build carries). A copy installed under another id isn't asked.
- **Cost.** Off, nothing runs. On, the tab being shared is asked once when its address changes, three more times over the next 25 seconds (its script and its reading of the episode come after the page does), and then not at all unless MAL-Sync answered with something, which is asked about every 15 seconds. A tab MAL-Sync has no script in costs four questions per page and no timer afterwards. Nothing is asked while nothing is being shared: a private tab that's paused, a browser page, or the browser out of focus past its Idle Timeout.
- **What's kept.** The reply is parsed and bounded like a PreMiD Activity's (`browser/src/premid/presence-data.ts`) and follows the same Settings > Privacy choices: thumbnails off drops the series cover, Media off drops the title, episode, buttons, and times, and Share Media Details off drops those and the cover too (it is the series), and either way the name falls back to "MAL-Sync". Only the Discord Applications MAL-Sync itself uses (its own, and its Anime and Manga ones) are accepted; a reply naming another is turned down whole, so MAL-Sync's pictures (its logo, play and pause icons, which are keys in those Applications) show. A link to the site being watched (MAL-Sync's "website" setting can make its button the stream's own address) is dropped, since a page's address stays in the browser. A timestamp that only drifts by under five seconds between questions isn't a change.
- **Two things to know.** Turn MAL-Sync's own "Discord Rich Presence" setting off if Discord-RPC-Extension is installed too: it doesn't affect Parousia (MAL-Sync answers whatever that setting says) but would show the same episode a second time through that extension. And MAL-Sync's other Discord settings (cover, activity name, buttons) still decide what it answers.

**Against the real extensions.** `bun run malsync:real` (Chromium and Firefox) runs the store builds of MAL-Sync and Discord-RPC-Extension, and Discord-RPC-Extension's own app, beside Parousia and Desktop, with stand-ins for Discord. MAL-Sync recognizes a served page of one of its sites and, with no network for its services, shows it as a "Local" entry. What it showed, and what that means for the Discord-RPC-Extension route that already existed:

- **That route needs Discord-RPC-Extension and its app, and Parousia isn't part of it.** MAL-Sync reaches Discord through them alone. Parousia's Discord-RPC-Extension support (its link to the app on port 6969) carries Parousia's own Activity, never MAL-Sync's, and its cross-extension protocol sends Parousia's presence to Discord-RPC-Extension, never the other way. Without Discord-RPC-Extension installed, MAL-Sync has no way to Discord at all, and with MAL-Sync support on it shows through Parousia Desktop.
- **Two presences nobody coordinates.** With Desktop connected, Parousia's own Activity (or the Default Activity) and MAL-Sync's, through the app, both show. With MAL-Sync support on, MAL-Sync's reply is Parousia's Activity for that page, one presence.
- **The app has one slot.** Without Desktop, Parousia's link and MAL-Sync (through Discord-RPC-Extension) both feed the app, which drops one client's presence to show the other's: 15 changes in a minute, here, between Parousia's Activity and MAL-Sync's. With MAL-Sync support on and MAL-Sync's own Discord setting off, the app got one steady presence.
- **Parousia's choices apply to one route.** Share Media Details off limits what Parousia shows of MAL-Sync (its name only), while the series title still went out through Discord-RPC-Extension, which Parousia can't limit.
- **Independent of MAL-Sync's Discord setting.** With it off, Discord-RPC-Extension showed nothing and Parousia still had the episode: MAL-Sync answers whatever that setting says.

What it didn't cover: a series MAL-Sync found on MyAnimeList (a cover, a View Anime button), a video playing (progress and pause), and the sites themselves. The Chromium run found that Chrome 153 defines `browser` as well, so telling Firefox from Chrome by that global asked Chrome for MAL-Sync under Firefox's id; both compat files now go by the extension's own address (`src/compat/browser-family.ts`).

## Discord-RPC-Extension

For users who prefer to use an established third-party application instead of the Parousia Desktop application, Parousia aims to remain compatible with Discord-RPC-Extension.

This allows users to use the Parousia browser extension with Discord-RPC-Extension as an alternative backend, without requiring the Parousia native application.

**Status:** Implemented. The extension talks to its app (`discord_rpc_ext`, port 6969) directly while Parousia Desktop isn't connected, on by default and switched in Settings > Platforms. Its cross-extension protocol is wired but inert until a Discord Application id is chosen for it.

[GitHub](https://github.com/lolamtisch/Discord-RPC-Extension)

## PreMiD Activities

Parousia supports the existing [PreMiD Activities](https://github.com/PreMiD/Activities) ecosystem rather than requiring every supported website to be reimplemented independently.

**Status:** Implemented. Parousia's build compiles PreMiD's Activities unchanged (1,411 of 1,417 when last built) and runs them on its own implementation of PreMiD's `Presence`/`iFrame` API, each as its own Discord Application. One is off until someone turns it on, which asks for access to its sites, and without them it doesn't run; what Activities may show from pages (what's playing, thumbnails, creator icons) is one choice in Settings > Privacy. Left out: Activities that need their own npm packages, target PreMiD's API version 2, or are on PreMiD's DMCA list. PreMiD's image service (`pd.premid.app`, which about a dozen Activities call to shorten a long image address or host a picture) is answered inside the extension and never contacted: a shortening gives the address back and an upload gives nothing, so the Activity shows without that image. An inline image (a `data:` address or a Blob, which PreMiD would host on that service) goes as the address of the picture it was made from when that's known: an `https` image drawn onto a canvas that's then exported, or a fetched image's Blob, like YouTube's thumbnail (`browser/src/premid/image-origin.ts`). One with no such address is left out, and the presence still shows. An Activity's activity type (Playing, Listening, Watching, Competing), which line the status shows (`statusDisplayType`), party size, and links on the images go through to Discord; the Streaming type has no equivalent and is dropped. PreMiD's translated descriptions (`description` in `metadata.json`) show in the languages Parousia's views are in. Not provided: `execInPage` with a function, `onRequest`, log reading, uploaded images, translated Activity strings, and Activities' npm packages. A service's own strings (`<service>.json`) are translated on PreMiD's Crowdin and served by its API, not kept in the Activities repository, and Parousia doesn't contact PreMiD, so `getStrings` answers in English and an Activity's language setting stays English. Five of the six Activities left out for needing npm packages (Netflix, HBO Max, U-NEXT and iFixit need `p-limit`, Emby `compare-versions`; the sixth, Claude, has a native Activity) are listed on the roadmap for native replacements: fetching and bundling third-party packages at build time would widen the supply chain beyond the Activities' own reviewed source (`project/threat-model.md`) for about 0.4% of the catalog. See `project/architecture.md`, PreMiD Activities.

[GitHub](https://github.com/PreMiD/Activities)

Parousia's own native Activities live in [parousia-project/activities](https://github.com/parousia-project/activities), filed the same way (`websites/<letter>/<Name>/`) and built through the same pipeline. A website with an Activity in both, under the same folder name, is listed once: the native one runs until someone picks PreMiD's on the Activity's page, and only the one chosen ever runs.
